Hellforge Rebalanced 1.0.7 — Patch Notes

2026-11-18 · Telair

A rebalance mod for HellForge: harder fights, snappier handling, and (almost) every weapon and stratagem worth taking.

Every change is listed below as old → new, grouped by weapon and enemy.

Color key: buff · nerf · minor adjustment.

Goals of this mod

Increased difficulty at the high end#

Higher difficulties get more combat pressure to make up for the equipment power creep of the last couple of years. The D7–D10 settings bring bigger encounter and guard budgets and more frequent, more simultaneous patrols (see Mission Difficulty). The original D10 settings now sit between D8 and D9, and D10 becomes a bridge to the HellForge D11 missions.

Snappier handling#

Game-wide changes to handling, ergonomics, sway, recoil, reload speed and ammo economy make weapons feel more responsive. Sway is lower on most weapons, and many weapons carry more spare ammo. Helldivers Survival Rework is a recommended partner mod that pushes this further.

A narrower power gap#

The gap between the most meta equipment and stratagems and the weakest is closed mostly by raising the underperformers. A few systems that weren't worth equipping, such as Stun Grenades, the Re-Educator and Shields, get light reworks.

More build variety#

More equipment can fill the utility role of plugging bug holes and interacting with mission objectives such as the Solo Silo. For example, the Plasma weapons, the Bolt Pistol, the Loyalist's charged shot and the Re-Educator can now destroy bug holes, bot fabricators and warp ships.

Limited, necessary nerfs#

A few systems warp the game's power balance too aggressively, and the Supply Bag is the main one: it now holds 2 supplies instead of 4. Ammo for the Ultimatum, Breacher, Missile Pistol, Grenade Pistol and stims was rebuilt around that change, so the totals stay similar.

Author's notes

Difficulty changes are designed to bridge the gap between the core Helldivers experience and the Hellforge Difficulty 11 missions in a more seamless way. Original D10 difficulty settings now live between D8 and D9, with D10 being a bridge difficulty between that and D11. These adjustments only impact enemy density and patrol density, through the settings the F8 menu exposes. You can change them if you'd like.

Balance changes are the culmination of ~600+ hours of gameplay testing in the last 2 months (as of release).

This system is not perfect; I am fallible and am open to community feedback. However;

I expect community feedback to be thoughtful, use specific enemy break points and equipment comparisons to justify the recommendations, and be open to discussion.

The HellForge Editor supersedes any changes made here. However, that also means that you have to scrub your Editor changes if you want my changes to apply. (Save your preset before clearing it!)

That's intentional; if you don't like a change I made, you can change it back. It's our game, we can play it how we want to.

I am intending to balance other creators' mods within the 'intended' power level of this system. So far, this does adjust the 0.4 release of Beelzebub's Stratagem Pack, Skyeshade's Stratagem Compendium, and the Scope of Authority Backport. I will add to this list as I get around to playtesting, but right now I am waiting for the 0.5 official release, which should significantly improve the quality and balance control of these kinds of mods.

Primary Weapons

Assault Rifles#

Balance Intent: Assault Rifles have lagged behind the meta-defining explosive primaries, fire/chemical weapons, and better performing shotguns for a long time. This created a meta where Light and Medium enemies were better dealt with by bringing a Stalwart or Machine gun, and bringing an explosive primary for the anti-Elite and utility (hole-plugging) roles, because ARs were generally not good enough to keep up.

Now they hit harder, handle better, and have the ammo necessary to deal with Light/Medium enemies, and are significantly more effective at Elite enemies in a pinch. They are generalist weapons which are rarely the best in slot for a role, but are often now effective enough to open up more build options by bringing one.

General Changes:

  • Damage: up on nearly every rifle.
  • Durable damage: up far more than standard damage, so rifles hit armored and heavy targets much harder.
  • Stagger: up on the mid and heavy rifles.
  • Fire rate: the Liberator family fires faster; the heavier rifles fire slower to balance their bigger damage.
  • Sway: lower on every rifle, so aim stays steadier.
  • Recoil: less horizontal recoil almost everywhere; the heavy rifles (Tenderizer, One-Two, MA5C) kick harder vertically.
  • Ammo: more spare and starting magazines on most rifles.
  • Reloads: faster across the board.
  • Attachments: muzzle device and underbarrel changes have their own sections below.

Exceptions:

  • AR-2 Coyote: the only ammo nerf (magazine 45 → 36 rounds); its damage change is durable damage and burn only.

Marksman Rifles#

Balance Intent: Marksman Rifles didn't have the benefits of DPM of AR/SMGs, the AOE damage of the explosive weapons, or the inherent durable damage of chemical/fire/plasma weapons. Even when requiring a higher level of accuracy to use, they often did not reward that accuracy with significant single target effectiveness.

Now they provide higher single target DPS vs heavy targets than Explosive/Plasma weapons, with better handling to make it easier to stay on target.

These changes are significant, but effectively carve out a place for Marksman Rifles to act as pseudo-heavy weapons for a variety of enemy types, rewarding accuracy and controlled engagements with ammo-efficient solutions to Elites and Heavy enemies from the primary slot.

General Changes:

  • Damage: significantly increased for most marksman rifles.
  • Durable damage: dramatically increased, so they hit heavy targets much harder.
  • Armor penetration: increased by one tier for most rifles.
  • Stagger: up on the Diligence pair and the Constitution; down on the Deadeye.
  • Fire rate: faster on the Constitution, Deadeye, Hyena and Hot-Shot; slower on the Counter Sniper.
  • Projectile: faster, flatter rounds on most rifles.
  • Sway: lower on every rifle, so aim stays steadier.
  • Recoil: less horizontal recoil everywhere it changed; more vertical drift on most rifles.
  • Ammo: fewer spare magazines or rounds on most rifles.
  • Reloads: rounds-reload weapons reload significantly faster.

Exceptions:

  • R-4 Hyena: the only damage nerf (220 → 180) and the only ammo buff; it also burns twice as hard.
  • R-6 Deadeye: lower velocity and higher drag push it toward a heavy-hitting mid-range niche.
  • R/40-K Hot-Shot Marksman Rifle: damage is up (275 → 320) and fire rate increased (210 → 300 rpm).

Submachine Guns#

Balance Intent: Submachine Guns had similar issues and solutions to Assault Rifles when compared to explosive weapons, while also being pretty underwhelming when compared to the ease of use of shotguns. Generally they received more ammo, better handling, better damage break points, and better stagger for high calibers.

In general, they are intended to reward close-medium range play with very significant DPM, especially against Durable enemies. I highly recommend mag-dumping the top half of a fleshmob with the Knight; it's no explosive crossbow, but it's surprisingly effective.

General Changes:

  • Damage: up on most SMGs, by half or more on the 12mm guns (Defender, Pummeler, Reprimand, Stoker).
  • Durable damage: up on every SMG, roughly doubled or more on most.
  • Stagger: up on the 12mm SMGs.
  • Fire rate: mostly unchanged; the Defender fires a little faster.
  • Projectile: much less drag on every SMG, so rounds keep their speed; the 12mm SMGs also fire faster rounds.
  • Sway: lower on every SMG, so aim stays steadier.
  • Recoil: much heavier on the Defender and Pummeler.
  • Ammo: the 12mm SMGs get smaller magazines but more spare magazines on most; the 9mm and StA SMGs also get more spare magazines.
  • Reloads: faster on nearly every magazine.
  • Magazine ergonomics: extended and drum magazines are lighter (−15 → −10).

Exceptions:

  • M7S SMG: the only damage nerf (90 → 75), traded for medium armor penetration.

Shotguns#

Balance Intent: Shotguns were a bit of a mixed bag, with a few standouts and many feeling slightly underwhelming when compared to the meta weapons. They are now defined into two fairly distinct roles; Shotguns which create space through high stagger (Punisher series), and close range DPM weapons (Breaker series). In general, the goal was to improve consistency of the weapon through higher pellet density, while also slightly improving damage break points at slightly longer range. The resulting changes create some very effective close range weapons, that generally trade either range or ammo economy to have best in class stagger or DPM for a primary weapon.

General Changes:

  • Damage: damage per shot is up on most shotguns; several now fire more, smaller pellets (Punisher, Spray&Pray, Incendiary).
  • Durable damage: up per shot on most shotguns; slightly down on the Incendiary and Double Freedom.

B-1 Supply Pack#

Balance Intent: The Supply Bag singlehandedly warps the entire game's ammo economy, especially for high power grenades and secondary weapons, around it. Previously this gave you access to 8 Ultimatum shots and 16 stims per supply call-in, making you functionally immortal and giving enough anti-tank ordnance to deal with anything in the game... all from just the secondary slot. This isn't even considering the anti-tank seeker grenades...

However that isn't why this is nerfed. In fact, the total for these weapons and grenades is similar post-changes. The problem was that the Supply Bag is functionally required to use those secondary weapons and grenades as your primary anti-tank solution, reducing build diversity dramatically. As such...

I've reduced it to 2, and intend to work with a mod developer who is better at creating game entities, because I'd like the supply bag to look "full" with the two shots. If you think you'd like to work on that project with me, feel free to reach out!

Before the comments complaining about this, keep in mind that I recommend this modpack with Helldiver Survival Rework, which cuts down on the 'stim tax' significantly, and most weapons that abused this have some form of compensatory change. You can also just edit the value back yourself, but I have rebuilt the ammo economy around this, so you will feel OP with the original supply bag.
